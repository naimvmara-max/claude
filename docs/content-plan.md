# Content plan

Paid ads are closed to this category on every major platform. Organic search, email and
direct outreach are the whole acquisition strategy, which makes content the primary growth
asset rather than a supporting one. Plan accordingly: this compounds over 6–18 months, not
6 weeks.

## The positioning in one line

**Everyone claims purity. Almost nobody explains the measurement.** The eight launch articles
all attack from that angle, because it is the only defensible position available — you cannot
compete on claims you are not allowed to make, and you should not want to.

After week 8, keep the cadence with the backlog below. A missed week costs more than a short post.

## Internal linking map

Link intentionally, not decoratively:

- **Every article → the pillar (article 1).** One contextual link in the first third.
- **Article 3 ↔ 4** (purity and identity are the same decision, split across two instruments).
- **Article 3 → 5** (purity vs content is the most-confused pair in the category).
- **Article 5 → 7** (net content is an input to the dilution math).
- **Article 6 → 7** (storage and reconstitution are one workflow).
- **Article 8 → 1, 2, 5** (the checklist references each check).
- **Every article → one relevant product or category page**, in the closing third, phrased as
  documentation ("the certificate for the lot in stock is on each listing"), never as a pitch.
- **Product pages → the two or three articles that explain their spec fields.** This is the
  half everybody skips, and it is the half that lifts conversion.

## On-page checklist per post

- [ ] Target query appears in the title, the URL slug and the first 100 words — once each, naturally
- [ ] Meta description written (the front matter carries it; copy into your SEO plugin)
- [ ] One `h1` (the title), `h2` for sections, `h3` only where genuinely nested
- [ ] At least one table or worked example — these earn featured snippets in technical queries
- [ ] Internal links placed per the map above
- [ ] Research-use line in the closing paragraph
- [ ] Passes `docs/copy-guidelines.md` with zero flagged terms
- [ ] Comments closed
- [ ] Submitted in Search Console after publishing

## Distribution

- **Search Console from day one.** Track impressions per query, not just clicks. Impressions
  move months before clicks do, and they tell you which article to expand.
- **Email.** Offer a "new lot certificates published" list on the COA lookup page. It is a
  legitimate reason to email regularly and it stays inside the copy rules.
- **Direct outreach.** Article 8 is the artefact to send a purchasing contact at a university
  or CRO. A useful checklist gets forwarded internally; a product catalog does not.
- **Forums and communities, carefully.** Answer analytical questions where researchers
  actually ask them, link only when the link genuinely answers the question, and never pitch.
  One promotional post undoes a quarter of goodwill.
- **Do not buy ads.** Meta, Google and TikTok prohibit the category. An account ban takes your
  business assets with it.

## The metric that matters

Not traffic. **Reorder rate and days-to-second-order.** This is a consumables business: a
customer who orders twice is worth many times a customer who orders once, and content's real
job is to make the first order feel like a reasoned purchasing decision rather than a gamble.
Track cohort reorder rate monthly from your first ten customers onward.
