# Content playbook

How the brand grows organically on TikTok and Instagram. Skills read this for formats
and pillars. Update it when something works twice.

## Pillars
1. **Build in public**: designing the brand with AI, step by step, with real numbers.
2. **Process**: from prompt to mockup to sample to product. Before/after is the format.
3. **Taste**: outfits, references, what is good and why. Opinions earn follows.
4. **Behind the hustle**: honest updates on money, mistakes, what is next.

## Cadence
- 1 short video per weekday, same time, cross-posted as TikTok + Reel.
- 2 carousels a week on Instagram (saves and shares).
- Stories daily: polls, questions, work in progress.
- Reply to every comment in the first hour.

## Hook patterns
| Pattern | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Process reveal | "I designed this hoodie with AI in 4 prompts" |
| Contrarian | "Stop buying blank hoodies from this supplier" |
| Mistake | "I lost €120 on my first sample. Here's why" |
| Before/after | "Prompt vs. real sample" |
| POV | "POV: you're 24 and building a clothing brand with AI" |
| Number | "Day 12 of building a brand from zero: 1,240 followers" |

## What to watch
- 3-second hold and average watch time: is the hook working?
- Shares and saves: is it worth passing on?
- Profile visits and follows per video: does it build the brand?
- Ignore likes on their own.

## AI content workflow
1. Idea from /hook-lab or /morning-intel's "Today's angle".
2. Visuals: Higgsfield for mockups, model shots and short clips, using the prompt base in [[brand-bible]].
3. Film the human part yourself (face, voice, hands). AI-only accounts grow slower and get trusted less.
4. Edit, label AI media, post, then run /content-cascade on it.
5. Log it in `content/posting-log.md`.
