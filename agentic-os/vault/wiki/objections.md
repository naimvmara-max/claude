# Objections

What Norwegian companies say, and answers that are true. /objections adds the ones heard in
replies and demo notes. Keep each answer short enough to say on the phone.

| Objection | Answer | Heard |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "Vi har allerede en chatbot." | "What share of questions does it answer without a person today? If it is under half, that is the gap we look at." | starter |
| "Kundene våre vil snakke med et menneske." | "The simple questions are answered at once, and everything else goes to your team with a summary, so people get a person faster." | starter |
| "For dyrt." | "Compared with what? One part-time person answering order status costs more per month. Let us count your questions first." | starter |
| "Ikke nå." | "Fair. When is a better time, and what has to happen before then?" Then set `next_date` to that date. | starter |
| "Send meg noe på mail." | "Gladly. So I send the right thing: what is the main question your customers ask?" | starter |
| "AI gjør feil." | "It can. That is why it only answers what it is set up to answer and hands the rest to you." (Check with `projects/product.md` that this is how ours works.) | starter |
