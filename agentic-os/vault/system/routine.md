# Routine

The Bridge copies the blocks under "Every day" into each new daily note's `## Schedule`.
/plan-today then adds calendar events and moves blocks around them. Edit freely.

## Every day

- 07:00 Plan the day, review directives
- 08:00 Gym
- 09:30 Deep work: client or income task (phone in another room)
- 12:30 Film or design: brand content
- 14:00 Edit and schedule today's post
- 16:00 Outreach: 10 personalised messages to potential clients
- 17:30 Engage: reply to every comment and DM
- 21:30 Close the day

## Notes

- Prayer times move through the year. If you want them as blocks, add your city here
  (for example `city: Stockholm`) and /plan-today will look the times up and insert them.
- One deep-work block on income every weekday, no exceptions. The brand compounds slowly;
  rent does not wait.
