# Routine

The Bridge copies the blocks under "Every day" into each new daily note's `## Schedule`.
/plan-today then adds calendar events and moves blocks around them. Edit freely.

## Every day

- 07:00 Plan the day, review directives and follow-ups due
- 08:00 Gym
- 09:30 Outreach: send drafted first emails and follow-ups
- 11:00 Prospecting: research new leads for tomorrow
- 13:00 Replies: answer every reply, book calls
- 14:00 Calls and proposals
- 16:00 Deep work: improve the offer, the list or the email copy
- 21:30 Close the day

## Notes

- Prayer times move through the year. If you want them as blocks, add your city here
  (for example `city: Stockholm`) and /plan-today will look the times up and insert them.
- Send during business hours on weekdays. Replies are what matter, and people reply at work.
