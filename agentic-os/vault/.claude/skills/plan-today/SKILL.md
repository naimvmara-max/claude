# Plan today

## Read first
`ops/directives.md`, `ops/pipeline.md`, `system/business.json`, `system/routine.md`,
today's note in `daily-notes/`, and today's events in Google Calendar if available.

## Do
1. Work out the pace: deals won this month vs `goal_deals_month`, workdays left, and how many
   new contacts a day are needed at the current rates (own rates once there are 100 contacts,
   5 meetings and 5 demos; otherwise the `assumptions`). Say plainly if the goal is out of reach
   this month.
2. Write `## Fokus` (3 items) for today: usually contacts (emails + calls), follow-ups due,
   and demos or offers.
3. Write `## Schema`: the routine blocks, calendar events (demos first), and a calling block
   at a time Norwegian businesses answer (09:00 to 11:30, 13:00 to 15:00).
4. Under `## Uppgifter`: follow-ups due, offers to make in the CRM, companies to research.

## Never
- Never create or change calendar events.


## Language
Write everything for Naim (the output file and your final reply) in Swedish. Anything for a customer is Norwegian bokmål.

## Output
Today's note. Reply with today's contact target and the first thing to do.
