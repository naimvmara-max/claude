# Product research

## Do
1. **Demand**: search interest trend, TikTok/Instagram content volume and engagement for the product, marketplace reviews. Note dates.
2. **Competition**: 5 stores or ads selling it, their price, angle and how long the ads have run (use ad libraries if available).
3. **Supply**: 2-3 suppliers with unit cost, shipping time to Scandinavia and the EU, and quality signals.
4. **Margin**: price − (unit + shipping + fees + expected ad cost per sale + 10% returns). Show the maths. Say clearly if it only works with organic traffic.
5. **Risks**: shipping times, IP or safety issues, platform restrictions, seasonality.
6. **Verdict**: Test, Maybe, or Skip, with the one reason that decides it. If Test, the smallest test that would prove it (budget, days, success number).

## Rules
- Most dropshipping products fail. Say "Skip" when the numbers say so.
- Never present supplier or "winning product" tool claims as fact without a source.

## Output
`outputs/product-research/YYYY-MM-DD-<slug>.md`. Reply with the verdict and margin.
