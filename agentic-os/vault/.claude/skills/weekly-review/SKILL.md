# Weekly review

## Do
1. From `ops/pipeline.md`: contacts per channel, reply rate (email), meetings booked, demos held,
   deals won, commission earned and paid this week and month to date.
2. Conversion: contact -> meeting, meeting -> demo, demo -> offer, offer -> signed. Compare with last week and with
   the `assumptions` in `system/business.json`. Say honestly whether the sample is big enough
   to mean anything.
3. Pace: deals needed for the month and contacts a day at the current rates.
4. Lost reasons this week (notes on lost rows). Which industry or channel worked best.
5. One change for next week based on the numbers (targeting, opener, follow-up timing, demo).
6. Rewrite `## Den här veckan` in `ops/directives.md` with three directives.


## Language
Write everything for Naim (the output file and your final reply) in Swedish. Anything for a customer is Norwegian bokmål.

## Output
`outputs/weekly-review/YYYY-MM-DD.md`. Reply with the week in two lines and next week's main directive.
