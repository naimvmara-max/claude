# Report to the manager

## Read first
`ops/pipeline.md`, `ops/calls.md` (call attempts from the Ring view), `ops/directives.md`,
the daily notes for this week, `projects/sales-job.md` (open questions) and the latest
`outputs/weekly-review/` file if there is one.

## Do
1. **Veckan i siffror** (Monday to today): new companies contacted (email and phone), call
   attempts and how many reached the right person, meetings booked, demos held, offers sent,
   deals signed (count and package names). Every number from the files; unknown = `okänt`.
2. **Det som funkade**: one or two concrete things, with the numbers behind them (a channel,
   an industry, an opener, an hour that reaches people).
3. **Det som bromsar**: deals signed but not activated yet, questions waiting for Driv
   (`fråga Driv` items), anything Naim needs from the manager. Say it plainly.
4. **Nästa vecka**: the three focuses from `ops/directives.md`, or a proposal if they are empty.
5. Swedish, short (under 200 words), the tone of a colleague reporting. Company names are fine;
   no contact persons, no personal email addresses, no notes about people.
6. With a Gmail tool, create a **draft** with the subject `Veckorapport v.<week> – Naim` and
   no recipient: Naim adds his manager and sends it himself. Without Gmail, the output file is
   the deliverable.

## Never
- Never send. Never put a recipient address you have not been given.
- Never include commission amounts, his pay or the runway figures: those are his own.
- Never present a guess as a number.

## Language
Write everything in Swedish: the report goes to his manager at Flowtel in Sweden.

## Output
`outputs/chef-rapport/YYYY-MM-DD.md`. Reply with the three numbers that matter most this week.
