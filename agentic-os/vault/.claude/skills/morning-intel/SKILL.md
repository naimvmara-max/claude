# Morning intel

## Do
Search the web for the last 2 days and keep only what matters for selling to Norwegian companies:
1. **Buying signals**: Norwegian companies that just posted customer service jobs (FINN jobb,
   arbeidsplassen.nav.no), opened new stores, launched online shops or had customer service
   trouble in the news. 3 at most, each with a link. Add them to `inbox/leads.md`, one per line.
2. **AI customer service in the Nordics**: competitors to Driv Kundesenter (for example
   Boost.ai and other chat, helpdesk and AI vendors), prices, launches, and news from Lynes,
   regulation (EU AI Act, Norwegian data rules).
3. **One sales idea** to try today, based on something above.

Every bullet: one line, the fact, why it matters, the source. No filler. If nothing relevant
happened, say so in one line.


## Language
Write everything for Naim (the output file and your final reply) in Swedish. Anything for a customer is Norwegian bokmål.

## Output
`outputs/morning-intel/YYYY-MM-DD.md` as bullets. Reply with the single most useful item.
