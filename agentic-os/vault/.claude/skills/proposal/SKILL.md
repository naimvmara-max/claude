# Proposal

## Input
Call notes or a lead briefing from `outputs/lead-research/`. If neither exists, ask for the client name and their main problem in the summary and stop.

## Do
1. Restate the client's goal and problem in their words.
2. Offer three options (good, better, best) with what is included, timeline and monthly price. Use the base offer in `projects/income-now.md`.
3. Show what success looks like after 30 and 90 days in numbers they can check (posts published, views, profile visits, enquiries). No promises of sales.
4. Next step: one clear action with a date.
5. If a Gmail tool is available, create a draft email with a short note and the proposal in the body. Do not send.

## Output
`outputs/proposal/YYYY-MM-DD-<client>.md`, plus the Gmail draft if possible. Update "Proposals out" in `projects/income-now.md`. Reply with the recommended option and price.
