## The honest math

Cold email is a numbers game with a slow start. Typical results are a few percent of
emails getting a reply and a fraction of those turning into calls. The dashboard shows
your real rates after a few hundred sends; plan on that, not on best-case screenshots.

| Weekly target | Number |
| --- | --- |
| First emails sent | 100 (20 per weekday) |
| Follow-ups sent | every lead, day 3, 7 and 14 |
| Calls booked | track it, then raise the bar |
