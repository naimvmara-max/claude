# Clothing brand

Status: building · Owner: Naim · Channels: TikTok, Instagram · Store: Shopify (not live)

## Approach

AI does the heavy lifting on design exploration, mockups, model shots and edits.
Naim does the taste, the story and the community. Content documents the build in public
from day one, so the audience exists before the first drop.

## Milestones

| # | Milestone | Done when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Brand bible | `wiki/brand-bible.md` has name, audience, voice, 3 visual rules |
| 2 | 30 days of posting | 30 posts in `content/posting-log.md` |
| 3 | First 1,000 engaged followers | vitals show 1,000+ on either platform |
| 4 | Pre-launch list | 200 people on a waitlist or close-friends list |
| 5 | Drop 1 | 1-3 pieces, print-on-demand or small batch, planned with /drop-plan |
| 6 | Sell-through review | margin per piece known, decision on drop 2 |

## Rules

- No inventory before demand: print-on-demand or pre-order until a design proves itself.
- Label AI-generated model shots as AI. Trust is the brand.
- Check every AI design for accidental copies of existing logos or artwork before it ships.
