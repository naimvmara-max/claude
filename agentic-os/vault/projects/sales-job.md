# The sales job

Naim sells an AI customer service platform to companies in Norway for his employer.
Commission only to start. This note holds what was said and what still has to be in writing.

## What I know so far (from the interview)

- Product: AI customer service hub. A chat widget on the customer's website and AI agents that
  work together, answer simple questions (opening hours, returns, order status), summarise
  conversations and pass the rest to staff. Details in [[projects/product]].
- Market: companies in Norway. The manager's view is that Norway is a year or two behind on AI.
  Treat that as a sales story to check, not a fact: many Norwegian companies are digitally mature.
- Pay: commission per deal, once.

| Package | My commission (NOK, once) | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Small | 5 000 | as I understood it, confirm |
| Medium | 10 000 | as I understood it, confirm |
| Large | 20 000 | as I understood it, confirm |

  The same amounts are in `system/business.json`. When the contract confirms them (or
  changes them), update that file and set `"confirmed": true`.
- I may use my own tools and lead lists to find companies.
- Mentioned: a base salary may start after about two months, with lower commission.

## Before I sign
- [ ] Exact commission per package (Small, Medium, Large) in NOK
- [ ] When commission is paid: when the customer signs, or when the customer has paid
- [ ] What happens to my commission if a customer cancels early (clawback)
- [ ] Employment or freelance (in Sweden: F-skatt, holiday pay, pension, who pays social fees)
- [ ] Base salary after two months: the amount, and how much the commission drops
- [ ] Written OK to use my own outreach tools and lead lists
- [ ] Which email address and domain I send from, and the rules for marketing email and calls
- [ ] Who owns the leads and customers I bring in if I leave
