## In one sentence

An AI customer service hub for Norwegian companies: an AI chat on your website and AI agents
that answer the simple questions customers ask every day, summarise the rest and hand it to
your team.

## What it does (as described at the interview)
- Chat widget on the company's website, answering around the clock
- AI agents that work together and pass information between them
- Answers simple, repeated questions: opening hours ("when do you open today?", "when do you
  close on Monday?"), returns ("how is my return going?"), order status ("where is my order?")
- Summarises conversations so staff only handle what needs a person

## Who it fits best
Companies that answer the same questions many times a day: online shops (order status,
returns), clinics, gyms, hotels, restaurants, workshops, property managers, travel and
ticketing. Signals: they are hiring customer service staff, have a long FAQ, short phone
hours, or reviews about slow replies.
